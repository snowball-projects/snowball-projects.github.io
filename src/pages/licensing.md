# Licensing and identity

## Software

snowball software is released under the [Apache License
2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) unless its source repository
states otherwise. The license permits use, modification, redistribution, and
commercial use subject to its terms. Third-party software and data retain their
own licenses.

## Attribution

Each source repository is canonical and carries its applicable license and
notices. Distributions and derivative works must preserve them as the licenses
require. Modified versions should state that they were changed and should not
imply that snowball produced or approved those changes.

## How snowball is built

**Built by AI agents**

snowball's founder, Nas Delevski, directs product and publication decisions. AI
agents carry out snowball-specific production work, including research, design,
code, testing, documentation, and writing. Third-party
software, data, models, infrastructure, and contributions retain their own
attribution. snowball remains responsible for what it publishes.

## Names and visual identity

The software license does not grant rights to the snowball, modo, or fairway
names, logos, or other identifiers beyond accurate references to a work's
origin. Forks and derivatives should use distinct identities and must not imply
official origin, sponsorship, or endorsement.

This does not limit rights provided by law or claim that any identifier is
registered.

## Official services

Software licenses govern source code, not access to snowball-operated
infrastructure. An official hosted service may publish a separate service
policy with privacy, resource, and acceptable-use limits.
