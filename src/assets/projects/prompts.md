# snowball project icons

These source images are 1254 × 1254 transparent PNGs. The prompts below preserve the original briefs, including the requested dimensions. Use the selected images as style references for future additions.

## modo

Source: `modo.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original project icon for modo, a road meeting-region tool that minimizes the longest drive among several origins.
Scene/backdrop: a genuinely transparent background with a real alpha channel. Square 1024×1024 PNG.
Subject: a small softly folded pale-blue paper map. Exactly three very simple dark curved route strokes approach from different parts of the map and converge at one warm ivory round meeting dot. Their lengths and approaches are visibly unequal. Use bold simplified folds, two or three broad map panels, and very little cartographic information. The ivory meeting dot is round, modest and slightly off center. This expresses finding a place to come together; it must not imply a geometric midpoint or equal travel.
Style/medium: one small tactile editorial illustration, a bold rounded charcoal outer contour, gently shaded matte surfaces with very subtle grain, restrained colors, warm and playful without a face. Strong simple shapes reminiscent of a friendly illustrated snowball mark; no snowball or swirl. View: mild overhead three-quarter view, compact centered form. Soft light from upper left. Simplify for excellent recognition at 48–56 pixels on an almost-black #0b0e0f website, card surface #101416. The color-bearing object fills about 74% of the square's width and about 74% of its height; ample clear transparent padding. Thick rounded contours and no hairline details.
Composition: the entire map is visible, with gently rounded corners and a lively folded silhouette. Pale blue panels, charcoal routes and outline, a single warm ivory dot. Treat the map as one coherent tactile object.
Text: none.
Avoid: letters, words, numbers, labels, watermark, faces, hearts, pins, compass, road grids, location names, arrows, scenery, mockups, square tile, badge, border frame, background color, checkerboard baked into the image, cast shadow outside the object, extra decorative objects.
```

## fairway

Source: `fairway.png` (revision 2). The original source is retained in Git history.

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: fairway project icon, square transparent PNG, clear at a 52px project-card size and a 32px favicon size.
Input images: Image 1 (fairway.png) is the edit target. Image 2 (modo.png) is a style reference only; do not alter modo and do not copy its three-route diagram.
Primary request: Revise fairway into one minimal, recognizable folded emerald-green map with an ivory golf flag marking the shared destination. The icon must communicate golf and travel routes together.
Subject and composition: Replace the existing putting-green mound and separate ball with a small open folded map. Use three broad pale emerald-green panels with a simple folded silhouette and bold rounded charcoal outline. Draw exactly TWO simple continuous dark charcoal curved routes, each approaching from a different origin on the map and both ending at the base of the SAME ivory golf flag. Make the two routes clearly unequal in length, with a loosely asymmetric convergence so the destination does not suggest an exact midpoint. Routes have plain rounded starts, without separate location markers. The flag has a short bold dark pole, an easily readable ivory triangular pennant, and a dark outline. Keep all parts visually joined as one object. Keep the flag large enough to read at 32px without overwhelming the folded-map silhouette.
Style/medium: Match the modo reference's strong rounded dark edging, restrained dimensional folded panels, soft matte tactile material, very mild paper-like texture and gentle shading. Use pale green map faces, subtle deeper emerald fold shading, charcoal routes and outlines, and a warm ivory flag. Keep the shapes broad, clean and sparse for tiny sizes.
Framing and background: One centered isolated icon with balanced clear margin, square canvas. Genuinely transparent background with alpha; no checkerboard pattern, no opaque background, no floor shadow.
Constraints: Exactly two curved routes and one golf flag. No separate ball, clocks, cars, text, letters, numerals, extra symbols, extra markers, golf clubs, terrain detail, grid, road network, scenery, badge, enclosing tile or platform. Do not reproduce modo's third route or circular central junction. Preserve the existing icon family's matte appearance and charcoal/ivory accents while changing fairway's main form to the green folded map.
```
